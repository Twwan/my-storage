This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application My Storage: Warehouse, developed and distributed through Google Play and the App Store.

The app helps you keep track of items at home, in the garage, at a warehouse, and in other storage locations. You can create storage places, zones, and containers, add items with photos, and mark them with QR or barcodes. Registration is not required, and your inventory data is not synced to our servers.

1. Data Collection and Use

We do not collect or receive on our servers the personal data you enter into the app (storage names, item descriptions, photos, quantities, QR/barcode values, etc.).

Personal data means any information that can be used to identify a person, such as: name, email address, phone number, location data, device information, etc.

We do not use, transfer, or sell your inventory data to third parties. You are responsible for the information you enter about your belongings and storage locations.

2. Local App Data

The app stores on your device the data needed for its work, for example: warehouses and storage locations, zones and containers, item records, attached photos, QR and barcode values, export files you create, and app settings.

This data is stored only on your device and is not transferred to us. You can delete it by removing records in the app or by uninstalling the app from your device.

3. Analytics (AppMetrica)

The app uses AppMetrica (Yandex) to collect anonymous analytics and usage statistics, such as: screen views, selected app settings (theme, accent color, card layout, photo display), and aggregated usage events (for example, creating a warehouse, scanning a code, or changing settings).

Item names, descriptions, photos, warehouse contents, and QR or barcode values are not sent to analytics. We do not show advertising in the app.

For more information, see the Yandex Privacy Policy.

4. Security

The app can be protected with a passcode and biometric unlock (Face ID, Touch ID, or equivalent). Passcodes and biometric data are processed by your device and are not sent to us.

Because your inventory data stays on your device, you are responsible for keeping your device secure.

5. Consent to the Privacy Policy

By using our App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

6. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to make changes to this Privacy Policy at any time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy on this page.

These changes take effect immediately after they are published.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us:

Email: twwan@inbox.ru
